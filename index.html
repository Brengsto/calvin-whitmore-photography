What I believe, in five lines

  1. Light is cheap. Patience isn’t.
  2. I take pictures.I wait.
  3. I don’t pose people. I hold my position.
  4. A forced smile is a false one.
  5. A good picture explains nothing.
About me, briefly

Born with wind in my face.

Kiel, 1984. Trained as a darkroom technician, then ten years at a local paper: village fairs, council meetings, a moose on the B 76.

That’s where I learned that every picture has a story if you stand still long enough. Today I work out of a backyard studio in Ottensen, with two cameras, one darkroom and no hurry. I shoot for magazines, for people who don’t like being photographed, and for companies that don’t want to look like an ad.

I deliver digital files too. But every picture starts on film. It slows me down, and that’s the point.
